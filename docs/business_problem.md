# Business Problem

## Problem Statement
Predict whether a newly created NYC 311 complaint will miss its expected resolution target.

## Intended Users
City operations managers, agency supervisors, and complaint-routing teams.

## Operational Action
High-risk complaints may be prioritized, escalated, reassigned, or monitored more closely.

## Prediction Moment
The prediction is generated shortly after complaint creation.

## Prediction Output
A probability and risk category.

## Business Success Criteria
Identify at least 70% of complaints that eventually miss their target while keeping the review workload manageable.

## Scope
Include selected agencies and a limited time range.

Exclude advanced modelling, deployment, and all-agency support from the first baseline.
